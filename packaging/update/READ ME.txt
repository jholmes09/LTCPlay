LTC Player update

There is one thing to do in here:

    Right-click 'Apply this update.command' and choose Open.

It finds the installed copy on this Mac by itself, keeps the old one, copies
the new program in, proves it with the self test, and puts everything back
exactly as it was if anything fails.

It does NOT touch your show file, your sequences, your saved audio input or
your Python environment.

Everything else this update contains is in '_payload'. Nothing in there is
meant to be run from this folder; it is copied into the install.

AFTER IT FINISHES

  Open the install, and in the Tools folder run
  'Set the Advatek triggers.command'.

  That is the part the update is not allowed to do for you, because the show
  file is yours.
